  waveform data, assignment PDFs, and teaching templates retain their course
  provenance. Their locations and verification records are listed in the
  [simulation input inventory](SIMULATION_INPUTS.md) and each homework's manifest.
- Copies named `snpreader.py`, including the supplied HW2 reference copies,
  retain this attribution. HW11's `legacy_snpreader.py` is a renamed copy of the
  same course helper; the name identifies its role as reference code.
- Rewritten solvers, verification code, generated results, and anonymous reports
  are separate coursework implementations. Their changes and numerical results
  should not be attributed to Prof. Chou or presented as his endorsement.
- Bundled third-party software, including ngspice, retains its own authorship,
  notices, and licenses. The course credit does not replace those notices or
  assert that third-party software was authored by the instructor.

Original reference files are preserved where possible; manifests document any
renaming, lossless compression, or anonymization. This attribution does not
assign a new blanket license to the supplied course materials. Student identity
is omitted from the rewritten reports; the instructor's public attribution is
intentionally retained.
